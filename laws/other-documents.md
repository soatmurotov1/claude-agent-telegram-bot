# Other documents of the questionnaire

- Extract from the state register (ЕГРПО / Ягона давлат реестри кўчирмаси): issued through the state services
  centres or my.gov.uz; shows the company name, STIR (ИНН), legal form, address, head, founders and, when there
  are any, encumbrances or arrests. Business registration follows Cabinet of Ministers resolution No. 66
  (09.02.2017, https://lex.uz/docs/3111342) as amended. The questionnaire asks for a "fresh" extract: the
  organisation accepts extracts issued no more than 3 months ago (the organisation's own rule, not a legal term).
- Environmental expert review: since 14.08.2026 it follows the law ZRU-1036 (24.02.2025) and resolution No. 234
  (11.05.2026). The questionnaire still names resolution No. 541 (07.09.2020), which governed it before. A
  conclusion is valid 3 years for EIA materials (ЗВОС/ЗЭП, ОВОС) and 5 years for environmental standards
  projects (ПДВ, ПДС, waste limits). Details are in company.md.
- Annual geological report form 5-ГР is submitted to the State Fund of geological information; proof is the
  report with an acceptance mark, a receipt or a letter.
- Land allotment for an industrial site, quarry or wells: a decision of the district / regional khokim
  (ҳоким қарори / решение хокима) or a land lease agreement, with the area and location of the plot (Land Code
  and Urban Planning Code). Under resolution No. 133 the land lease agreement is part of the subsoil use
  conditions.
