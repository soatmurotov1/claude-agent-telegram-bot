# Law ZRU-57 "On Industrial Safety of Hazardous Production Facilities" (Закон «О промышленной безопасности опасных производственных объектов»), 28.09.2006

Source: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1061184 (read 27.10.2026), as amended by ZRU-373 (2014), ZRU-456 (2018) and later laws.

- Art. 5. Hazardous production facilities (ОПО / хавфли ишлаб чиқариш объектлари) must be entered in the state
  register of hazardous production facilities kept by the authorised industrial safety body. A registration
  certificate or register extract proves this.
- Art. 15. Industrial safety expert review (ЭПБ / саноат хавфсизлиги экспертизаси) assesses: project documents
  for building, extending, reconstructing, re-equipping, mothballing or closing the facility; technical devices
  used there; buildings and structures; the industrial safety declaration and other operating documents.
  It is done by organisations accredited for it, at the cost of the operator. The result is an expert review
  conclusion (заключение экспертизы) with a number, a date, the expert organisation and its accreditation.
- Art. 19. Facilities with hazardous substances above the limits must have an industrial safety declaration,
  approved by the head of the operating organisation.
- Resolution No. 784 (02.10.2018, see company.md): technical devices older than 20 years or past their service
  life must pass expert review; the expert organisation sends the conclusion to the authority within 2 days.
- Permits for mining, blasting and oil and gas facilities are issued by the Inspectorate for control in mining
  and geology (formerly «Саноатгеотехназорат»), see resolution No. 169 in company.md.
