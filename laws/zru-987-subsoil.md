# Law ZRU-987 "On Subsoil" (Закон «О недрах», «Ер қаъри тўғрисида»), 31.10.2024

Source: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7182163 (read 27.10.2026). In force since 02.02.2025; replaced the 2002 law.

- Art. 31. Every kind of subsoil use needs a permit for the right to use subsoil plots (разрешение на право
  пользования участками недр / ер қаъри участкаларидан фойдаланиш ҳуқуқига рухсатнома). It is issued by the
  state institution "Subsoil Use Centre" (Центр пользования недрами / Ер қаъридан фойдаланиш маркази); permits
  for underground storage and burial of waste are issued by the Ministry of Ecology. The plot size is given in
  cadastral squares. Permits are issued "first come, first served" or by e-auction / tender.
- Art. 32. Applicants and holders: citizens of Uzbekistan, residents, legal entities registered in Uzbekistan.
  A physical person not registered as an entrepreneur may not hold a permit (except collection samples).
- Art. 42. Geological study permit for solid minerals: main minerals up to 5 years (can be extended twice by 4
  years), non-metallic (нерудные) minerals up to 2 years (can be extended twice by 2 years). At each extension the
  number of cadastral squares is reduced by 50%.
- Art. 48. Geological study holders report every year on their obligations for the previous year by 1 March;
  a final report is due within 3 months after the permit ends.
- Art. 49. Reserves are approved by the State Commission on Mineral Reserves (ГКЗ / Давлат захиралар
  комиссияси); its decision puts the reserves on the state balance.
- Art. 53. Before a mining permit, the Centre reviews the deposit development project or the feasibility study
  (ТЭО / ТИА).
- Art. 54. A mining permit is valid for at most 25 years from issue and can be extended repeatedly, each time
  for no longer than the initial term.
- Art. 61. Mining holders submit an annual mining report (works, extracted minerals and losses, geological
  report, sales and costs, annual financial statements) to the Centre by 31 March of the next year.
- Art. 110. An extension is applied for within the last year before the permit expires.
