# Law ZRU-598 "On Investments and Investment Activity" (Закон «Об инвестициях и инвестиционной деятельности»), 25.12.2019

Source: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4664144 (read 27.10.2026), with later amendments.

- Art. 6. Forms of investment include founding a legal entity or buying a share in its charter capital,
  buying property, shares or securities, and acquiring concessions, including for the exploration, development
  and extraction of natural resources. This matches the deal formats in item 10 of the questionnaire (sale of a
  share, joint venture, sale of 100% of the assets).
- Art. 15-22. The state guarantees investors' rights and non-discrimination, free use of income after taxes,
  free transfer of foreign currency into and out of Uzbekistan (including repatriation of income and sale
  proceeds), the return of foreign investments when the activity ends, and protection from unfavourable changes
  of legislation.
- For the check of item 10: a claimed independent valuation must be a report of a valuation or audit
  organisation (name, number, date, signature). Court disputes and pledges of the licence or assets with banks
  lower the attractiveness of the site and must be stated truthfully.
