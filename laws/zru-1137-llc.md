# Law ZRU-1137 "On Limited Liability Companies" (Закон «Об обществах с ограниченной ответственностью»), 21.04.2026

Source: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8152146 (read 27.10.2026). In force since 22.07.2026; replaced law No. 310-II of 2001.

- Art. 12-13. The founding documents of an LLC (МЧЖ / ООО) are the charter and, when there are several founders,
  the founding agreement (учредительный договор).
- Art. 14. The charter (Устав / Устав) must contain: the full and short company name; the subject of activity;
  the postal address; the bodies of the company and their powers, including the exclusive powers of the general
  meeting and how decisions are taken; the size of the charter capital (уставный фонд / уставный капитал);
  the size and nominal value of each participant's share; participants' rights and duties; how a participant
  leaves; how shares pass to others; how documents are kept and information is given; branches, representative
  offices, subsidiaries.
- The charter and every change to it are subject to state registration and take effect for third parties from
  registration. A charter without a registration mark (or a registration decision / QR code from the state
  services centre) is not the current registered version.
- Charters adopted under the old law stay valid, but the questionnaire asks for "the current version": a charter
  with later changes that are not included is not current.
